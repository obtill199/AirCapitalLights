Questions

Do I need to be home?

No. We text before we arrive. We just need an outdoor outlet we can reach.

What if a bulb goes out?

Text us and we’ll fix it within 48 hours, weather permitting. Storm damage or outlet problems are checked first, and we tell you about any cost before doing the work.

Who owns the lights?

Air Capital Lights does. We take them down in January and store them. Your price covers one season, and rebooking next year is up to you.

Do you do two-story homes, or lights I already own?

Yes. Both get a free custom quote. We look at your roofline (and your strands) and text you a price before you pay anything.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit holds your install date, and the rest is due on install day. Takedown is already included, so there’s no January bill.

When do lights go up and come down?

Installs run Nov 9 to Dec 15. Takedown is Jan 4 to Jan 24.
